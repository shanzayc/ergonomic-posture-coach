How it works

  1. Put your webcam in front of you, near eye level.
  2. Sit the way you want to sit — head and shoulders in frame.
  3. Start the session and allow camera access.
  4. The first 5 seconds calibrate to your neutral posture.
  5. Keep working. You'll be alerted if you drift, even on another tab.

Private by design. Pose detection runs locally in your browser. No video is uploaded or stored, and session stats stay on this device.
