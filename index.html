How it works

  1. Put your webcam in front of you, near eye level.
  2. Sit the way you want to sit — head and shoulders in frame.
  3. Start the session and allow camera access.
  4. The first 5 seconds calibrate to your neutral posture.
  5. Keep working. You'll be alerted if you drift, even while you're in another app.

Private by design. Pose detection runs locally in your browser. No video is uploaded or stored, and session stats stay on this device.

Accuracy test

Each round walks through the postures you pick: a 3-second countdown, then hold the posture for 7 seconds (beeps mark the start and end). Every frame is saved, then scored against the original rules and the current ones. Start a session and finish calibrating first.
